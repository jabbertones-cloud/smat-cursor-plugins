# Trademark Office Action evidence review

1. Identify official serial number, mark, issuing office, date and source record, not just the document title.
2. Classify only refusals and procedural requirements explicitly present in the supplied Office Action.
3. Quote brief source excerpts and cite page, record URL and date; flag missing notices or incomplete scans.
4. Separate examiner reasoning, applicant allegations and independent commentary.
5. Record deadline information only when confirmed in authoritative records; require official recheck for ambiguous deadlines.
6. Produce an issues/evidence matrix and research questions, not legal advice, a signed response or a filed submission.
