# Inventory reconciliation

1. Confirm store, product and variant identifiers, units, effective date and snapshot authority.
2. Estimate ending inventory from opening stock, receipts, transfers, sales, returns and adjustments where source evidence exists.
3. Separate estimated quantities from independently observed closing stock.
4. Flag duplicate movements, missing transfer events and SKU mismatches without asserting theft or shrink.
5. Present evidence, discrepancy amount, uncertainty and independent verification path.
6. Never create a retail transaction, promotion or adjustment through this free plugin.
